About Azomakhanye

Built on the plant floor, scaled across the continent.

Founded in 2007 by a team of mining engineers and tradesmen, Azomakhanye has grown from a single-discipline maintenance crew into a multi-discipline industrial services partner trusted by major mining houses.

We combine deep mechanical, electrical, automation and structural capability with rigorous planning and zero-harm safety culture.

  • B-BBEE Level 1 contributor
  • ISO 9001 / ISO 45001 aligned
  • 24/7 breakdown response across SA

Shutdown Management

A 5-phase shutdown methodology engineered for zero surprises.

  1. 01

    Scope & Strategy

    Workpacks, risk register, critical path defined 12 weeks out.

  2. 02

    Planning & Logistics

    Resource loading, spares staging, contractor mobilisation.

  3. 03

    Pre-shutdown

    Permits, isolations, site readiness audit.

  4. 04

    Execution

    24/7 supervised execution with live progress dashboards.

  5. 05

    Hand-over & Review

    Quality sign-off, post-mortem and learnings captured.
